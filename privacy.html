Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

This privacy policy explains how TradePad collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

What we collect

When you join our early access list, we collect your email address. We may optionally ask about your trade type to help us prioritise features.

How we use it

We use your email address solely to contact you about TradePad. This includes:

  • Notifying you when early access is available
  • Asking for feedback on product direction
  • Occasional product updates (no more than once per month)

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties. We do not use your email for advertising unrelated products. We do not track you across other websites.

Data storage

Your email is stored securely by Supabase, our database provider. Both we and Supabase are based in the UK/EU and comply with GDPR.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing founder@nirav.work. We will remove your data within 30 days.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at founder@nirav.work.
